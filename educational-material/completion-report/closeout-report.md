## Project Overview

- Delivered a complete, multilingual educational video library that onboards web2 developers into the Cardano and Midnight ecosystems
- Produced and published content in English, Spanish, and Portuguese, removing language as a barrier to participation
- Evolved content alongside the ecosystem itself, spanning the Testnet, Preview, and Mainnet-readiness eras of Midnight
- Coordinated closely with the Midnight Foundation to keep every episode technically accurate and aligned with current tooling
- Engaged the community continuously, adapting topics and timing in direct response to feedback from developers and partners

---

## Project KPIs and How the Project Addressed Them

- **Multilingual Video Production:** Produced and published 46+ episodes across three milestones in English, Spanish, and Portuguese — exceeding the original 44-episode commitment
- **Content Quality & Format:** Standardized on a 1-hour, 3-part format (Ecosystem Explainers → Coding/Build Session → Q&A Deep Dives) after a testing phase in Milestone 1
- **Production Workflow:** Established and operated a stable production pipeline using VS Code, OBS, Premiere Pro, camera, and microphone; team roles and review process defined and consistently applied
- **Supplementary Resources:** Released and maintained companion GitHub repositories (Midnight Starter Template, Midnight Contracts) and full educational-material documentation
- **Ecosystem Alignment:** Maintained active collaboration with the Midnight Foundation team across all three milestones; featured in the Midnight Awesome DApps catalog
- **Community Responsiveness:** In the final stretch, expanded the last 5 planned episodes into 7 community-requested episodes (~5h 31min total) to cover new tools and projects that emerged late in the program

---

## Key Achievements

- **Full library delivered:** 46+ published episodes covering the full Midnight developer journey, from environment setup to building production-grade privacy dApps
- **Three-language reach:** All episodes accessible in English, Spanish, and Portuguese — initially as three separate videos, later as single videos with embedded multi-language audio tracks for streamlined publishing
- **Format evolution validated:** The 1-hour, 3-part structure consistently received positive feedback from both community developers and the Midnight Foundation, becoming the standard format
- **Ecosystem evolution captured:** Series tracked Midnight from Testnet through the Preview network and into Mainnet-readiness, including major moments such as Compiler 0.27/0.28 releases, Glacier Drop, Trusted Node Operators, the Midnight Summit Hackathon, and the launch of the Aliit Fellows program
- **Build sessions across real projects:** Hands-on coding episodes covered the Bulletin Board, LunarSwap, Brick Towers RWA, Midnight Escrow Marketplace, SilentLedger DEX, DPO2U Compliance, ZK TOTP Authenticator, Starship Leaderboard, ZKLoan, Midnight Kitties, Bucket DeFi, and more
- **Tooling deep-dives:** Dedicated episodes on midnight-wallet-cli, compact-playground, the Compact language, Midnight Explorers & Indexers, Lace & 1AM Wallets, and the Midnames SDK / .night domains
- **Community-driven expansion:** Final 7 episodes (vs. the 5 originally planned) delivered in direct response to community requests, capturing tools and projects that emerged during the program
- **Featured externally:** Recognized in the Midnight Awesome DApps repository and consistently referenced by the broader Cardano and Midnight developer communities

---

## Key Learnings

- **A consistent format beats variety.** The early Milestone 1 testing across 30-min, 1-hour, and code-only formats made clear that a single 1-hour, 3-part structure produces the most engagement and is the most sustainable to produce
- **Multi-language audio in one video scales better than three separate videos.** Switching to embedded audio tracks in Milestone 2 simplified publishing, reduced channel fragmentation, and made the content easier for global audiences to find and share
- **The community is the best content planner.** Bi-weekly planning aligned to the Midnight and Cardano roadmaps — combined with active listening to developer questions — consistently produced more relevant episodes than any fixed editorial calendar would have
- **Responsiveness matters more than rigidity.** Adjusting the final 5 planned episodes into 7 community-requested topics added measurable value without compromising format or quality, and reinforced trust with the community
- **Hands-on build sessions are the highest-leverage content.** Episodes that walked through real contracts and real dApps consistently outperformed pure-explainer formats and gave web2 developers a credible on-ramp into ZK and privacy-preserving development
- **Close coordination with the Foundation is non-negotiable.** Continuous alignment with the Midnight Foundation team kept the content technically accurate during a period when the ecosystem (Compact, DevTools, Lace, Indexer, Wallet) was changing rapidly
- **Educational infrastructure compounds.** The companion repositories (Midnight Starter Template, Midnight Contracts) became reusable scaffolding for new developers, not just supporting material for the videos

---

## Next Steps for the Product

- **Maintain the library as a living reference.** Keep the YouTube catalog, GitHub repositories, and educational-material documentation up to date as the Midnight ecosystem continues to evolve toward and beyond Mainnet
- **Continue partner-driven content.** Build on collaborations established during the program (Midnight Foundation, OpenZeppelin, Brick Towers, Aliit Fellows, Nightforce Ambassadors) for follow-on content series
- **Grow the multilingual community.** Continue supporting Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking developers, who remain underserved in the broader ZK and privacy ecosystem
- **Leverage the Starter Template as an on-ramp.** Position the Edda Labs Starter Template as the canonical entry point for new web2 developers building their first privacy dApp on Midnight

---

## Final Thoughts / Comments

This project set out to make Midnight and Cardano accessible to web2 developers in their own language — and it delivered exactly that, across three milestones and 46+ episodes. We are particularly proud of the community-driven expansion in the final stretch: rather than closing the program on a fixed checklist, we listened to what the community was asking for and delivered more than was originally committed. The Midnight ecosystem grew dramatically over the course of this initiative, and we worked hard to make sure the educational library grew with it.

The collaboration with the Midnight Foundation team has been a defining strength of this work. Their input kept the content sharp, current, and credible, and helped position the Edda Labs channel as a trusted on-ramp into the ecosystem. We are also deeply grateful to the developers, testers, ambassadors, and Aliit fellows who showed up in the Q&A segments, suggested topics, and helped shape the direction of the series.

Thanks to Catalyst and the Midnight Foundation, this work now lives on as a durable, multilingual reference for anyone entering the Cardano and Midnight ecosystems. We look forward to building on this foundation with future content, integrations, and community programs.
